Audit of 8 November 2026. P0 would invalidate results. P1 are validation gaps. P2 strengthen credibility. P3 are research beyond the challenge.

## P3: research beyond the challenge

- Detection-probability model: model the chance that MODIS sees a fire VIIRS sees as a function of fire size (FRP), scan angle, land cover and cloud, using coincident Aqua and S-NPP overpasses (same 13:30 equator crossing). This replaces a ratio per place-month with a physically based correction and could follow regime changes.
- Distribution matching (quantile mapping) of cell-month values per region, compared against the ratio method on the same backward test.
- Fire radiative energy with a diurnal cycle model, so fire power becomes an energy that emission models can use.
- Event identification (spatio-temporal clustering of hotspots), so the calendar can count fires as well as hotspots.
- A pre-2012 bridge using a third sensor to test the factor before 2012 directly, for example research-level fire detection in Landsat-5/7 shortwave-infrared scenes for selected regions and dates (there is no standard Landsat active fire product before Landsat-8).
